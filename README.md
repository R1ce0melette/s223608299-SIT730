##Fire alarm for hearing impaired people - Task 4.2C

#Hardware:
•	Arduino Nano 33 IoT
•	1 LED
•	1 220 Ohm resistor
•	1 slide switch
•	1 DHT11 Temp & Humid sensor.
•	5 F-M wires
•	4 M-M wires.

LED is controlled by both the switch and the sensor. The sensor turn on the LED when a temperature threshold is passed. The switch can override the sensor when used. 

Overriding duration is 2 minutes
